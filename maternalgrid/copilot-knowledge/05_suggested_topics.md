# Suggested Copilot Studio Topics

Create these as topics with the listed trigger phrases. All answers come from the
knowledge sources; none require a live data connection.

## T1 — What is a maternity care desert
Triggers: "what is a maternity care desert", "define desert", "what does desert mean",
"maternity care desert definition"

## T2 — Georgia access statistics
Triggers: "how many counties lack maternity care", "how bad is it in Georgia",
"Georgia statistics", "how many deserts"
Note: there are three different measures — give all three with definitions.

## T3 — How travel time is calculated
Triggers: "how do you calculate travel time", "what is modeled travel time",
"is this real drive time", "how accurate is this"

## T4 — Why 30 minutes
Triggers: "why 30 minutes", "where does the threshold come from",
"is 30 minutes a clinical standard"
Note: it is a conservative operational threshold, NOT a clinical cut-point. Cite the tiers.

## T5 — Data sources and currency
Triggers: "where does this data come from", "how current is this", "what are your sources",
"can I cite this"

## T6 — What the tool can and cannot do
Triggers: "what can this do", "can it predict outcomes", "what are the limitations"

## T7 — Clinical advice redirect (MANDATORY)
Triggers: "should I deliver at", "is my pregnancy high risk", "which hospital is safest",
"what should I do", "I'm pregnant and"
Action: refuse and redirect per 04_scope_and_disclaimers.md. Never give individual guidance.

## T8 — Fallback
Answer only from the knowledge sources. If the answer is not there, say so explicitly
rather than generating one.
