# Verified Georgia Statistics

Every figure below carries its source and year. Three commonly confused metrics are
listed separately because they measure different things and must not be used
interchangeably.

## Access metrics — these are three DIFFERENT measures

| Metric | Figure | Share of 159 counties | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counties with no birthing facility | 108 | 67.9% | Georgia DPH, 2025 |
| Counties in OB/GYN shortage | 82 | 51.6% | GA Board of Health Care Workforce, 2024 |
| Counties classified Maternity Care Desert | 70 | 44.0% | March of Dimes, 2026 |

If asked "how many Georgia counties lack maternity care", ask which measure is meant, or
give all three with their definitions. Do not present one as though it were another.

## Births (Georgia DPH OASIS, births by county of residence)

- 2024 statewide births: **126,304**
- 2015 statewide births: **131,333**
- Change 2015 to 2024: **-3.8%**
- Births in the 70 desert counties (2024): **9,880**, which is **7.8%** of all Georgia births
- Median annual births, desert counties: **120**. Median elsewhere: **579**

## The access-decline loop

Desert counties are losing birth volume faster than the rest of the state: mean county
change 2015–2024 was **-4.0%** in desert counties against **+0.6%** elsewhere. Falling
volume undermines the financial viability of a rural labor & delivery unit, which drives
closure, which expands the desert. This is the core dynamic MaternalGrid exists to model.

## Facilities

- Georgia in-state delivering hospitals: **66** total, **62 active**, 4 closed units
  (Elbert Memorial, Washington County Regional, Dorminy Medical Center, Wayne Memorial).
- Maternal levels of care in-state: Level IV **7**, Level III **14**, Level II **24**, Level I **21**.
- Out-of-state border facilities serving Georgia residents: **11** identified, **9 active**
  (Tennessee, Florida, Alabama, South Carolina).
- Total active delivering facilities available to Georgia residents: **71**.

## Maternal mortality — two different measures

- **37.9 per 100,000 live births** — pregnancy-related mortality ratio, 141 deaths,
  2020–2022, Georgia DPH Maternal Mortality Review Committee. **87%** of those deaths had
  at least some chance of being prevented.
- **66.3 per 100,000** — a separate CDC/DPH maternal mortality figure for 2024, using a
  different definition and period.

These are not interchangeable. Quote one, name its definition and year, and do not average
or combine them.
