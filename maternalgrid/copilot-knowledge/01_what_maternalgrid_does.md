# MaternalGrid — What It Is

MaternalGrid is a scenario-simulation platform for maternity-care access in Georgia.
It answers one question quickly: **if this labor & delivery unit closes — or if we fund
a new one here — who loses access, by how much, and what does that cost in risk?**

## Who it is for
- **Georgia DPH analysts and leadership** — county drilldowns, capacity risk, resource allocation.
- **Nonprofit stakeholders and grantors** — defensible impact narratives for grant justification.

It is a **planning tool**, not a clinical tool. It models geographic access to obstetric
facilities. It does not predict health outcomes and does not advise individuals.

## What it can do
- Simulate closing an existing delivering hospital and show the regional effect.
- Simulate opening a new facility and show how many births regain access.
- Identify which neighbouring hospitals absorb displaced birth volume.
- Compare two policy options side by side across all 159 counties.

## What it cannot do
- Predict an individual's pregnancy risk or birth outcome.
- Recommend a hospital for a specific person.
- Report real-time bed availability or current facility operating status.
- Give routed drive times accounting for traffic, weather, or time of day.
